## ✦ About

I'm Srijan K, a creative designer and web developer focused on combining visual design with technology. My work sits at the intersection of **Design × Technology × Communication** — bringing together visual communication, campaign design, and web development into a single, consistent practice.

This portfolio itself is an example of that: every layout, color choice, and interaction on this site was designed and built by hand rather than assembled from a template.
